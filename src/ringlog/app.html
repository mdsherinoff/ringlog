Open an iPhone backup

Choose a backup in the sidebar to see its call history. Nothing leaves this Mac.

  1. Connect your iPhone with a cable, unlock it, and tap Trust.
  2. In Finder, select the iPhone, choose Back up all of the data on your iPhone to this Mac, then click Back Up Now.
  3. When it's done, click Refresh and the backup appears in the sidebar.
